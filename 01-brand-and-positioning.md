# Brand & Positioning

## What Cinekuts Is
Cinekuts is a curated film and TV review brand built around Vojtech's own taste, expressed through a companion website and a revived YouTube channel (@cinekutss). It positions itself against algorithmic aggregation sites by making curation and scoring explicitly human and editorial.

## Platform Hierarchy
**Website > YouTube > TikTok > Instagram**

The website is the canonical home for full reviews; YouTube is the primary content engine; TikTok and Instagram are secondary, platform-differentiated distribution channels (see `05-social-and-automation.md`).

## Legacy Channel Status
- Handle: @cinekutss
- ~11.5K subscribers, 31M legacy views
- Dormant for 450+ days prior to relaunch
- Old content was reposted clips with no commentary, which violates YouTube's reused-content policy — this caused a prior YouTube Partner Program (YPP) rejection
- The 17 old Shorts need to be deleted before YPP reapplication

## Core Identity: AI for Production, Never Taste
This is a non-negotiable credibility pillar and the brand's main differentiator:
- AI handles production tasks: voiceover, script drafting, graphics
- Taste, curation, and scoring are exclusively Vojtech's — never automated, never algorithmic
- The brand does **not** claim "100% human, no AI" (that would misrepresent the actual pipeline) — instead it draws an honest, explicit line between production tooling and curatorial judgment

## Competitive Landscape
| Competitor | What they do | Gap Cinekuts fills |
|---|---|---|
| TasteRay | Taste-matching recommendations | No proprietary scoring system, no video companion |
| A Good Movie to Watch | Curated recommendations | No scoring system, no YouTube presence |
| Criticker | User-driven taste profiling | Crowdsourced, not editorially curated |
| iCheckMovies | Watchlist/checklist tracking | Not review-driven, no scoring or video |

No competitor combines a distinct proprietary scoring system (Kuts Score) + mood-based discovery + an active YouTube companion channel.

## Content Model: Curated-Only
- Every published title has a real, written review — no unreviewed catalog filler
- `coming-soon` status is used sparingly, only for titles Vojtech has explicitly committed to reviewing
- The site deliberately does not flood itself with placeholder entries just to look comprehensive — quality and editorial integrity over catalog size

## Mood Tag Vocabulary
Fixed set of 14 tags used across the site for mood-based discovery (Phase 4):

slow-burn · feel-good · bleak · tense · funny · heartfelt · visually-stunning · mind-bending · nostalgic · disturbing · underrated · overhyped · popcorn · melancholic

## Content Format Rotation
Reviews/content rotate across three formats:
1. Straight review
2. Comparison (title vs. title)
3. Underrated vs. overrated
