# Social & Automation

## Comment-to-DM Automation
**InstantDM** was selected for keyword-triggered comment-to-DM automation — flat-rate pricing, and it can handle traffic spikes from giveaways.

## Giveaway Program
- **Format:** Cash via PayPal/Wise — not streaming gift cards
  - Gift cards were explicitly rejected due to severe region-lock issues; cash avoids that entirely
- **Cadence:** ~€12–13/week, four weekly draws per month
- **Entry requirements:** YouTube + TikTok follow required; Instagram follow is a bonus entry
- Winner announcement templates are already built

## Platform-Specific Content Strategy
Content is **not** cross-posted identically — each platform gets a distinct treatment:

| Platform | Treatment |
|---|---|
| TikTok | Rawest, most debate-bait-oriented edits |
| YouTube Shorts | Branded overlays, funnels viewers to the full channel |
| Instagram | Carousels and Stories prioritized |
| Website | Canonical full reviews (unaffected by social formatting) |

## TikTok Limitation: Organic Comment Automation
TikTok's Comments API only covers **paid ad campaigns** — it cannot be used for organic content. This makes comment-to-DM or comment-reply automation on organic TikTok posts impossible by platform design. Manual replies are the only viable path there.
